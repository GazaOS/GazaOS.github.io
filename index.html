What is GazaOS?

GazaOS combines the stability of LineageOS and Lunaris-AOSP with crDroid customization, HyperOS/ColorOS-inspired UI craft, and our own Gaza Lab and system logic—built openly on GitHub under the Apache 2.0 license.
